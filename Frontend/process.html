The science of less

Why freeze-dried, not just dried.

Freeze-drying removes water at sub-zero temperatures, preserving flavour, nutrients and shape — unlike heat or sun drying. The result: real food that looks, tastes and feels like the fresh original.

Step by step

From harvest to sealed pack

Each product goes through six controlled stages — the same process used by pharmaceutical and aerospace food suppliers.

01
Harvest & selection
Produce is picked at peak ripeness and sorted within hours — only fruit that meets standards moves forward.
02
Wash & prep
Everything is washed, peeled, and sliced to uniform thickness — consistency controls even drying.
03
Flash freeze
Blast-frozen to −40°C within minutes. Fast freezing creates tiny ice crystals that preserve texture.
04
Vacuum drying
In a sealed chamber, ice sublimes — turning directly from solid to vapour, removing ~95% of moisture.
05
Quality check
Every batch tested for moisture, colour and safety. Products receive a batch code tracing back to source.
06
Pack & seal
Packed in moisture-barrier pouches, sealed, graded, and labelled with batch code and best-before date.
