The process

Less water. Same food.

Freeze-drying pulls moisture out at sub-zero temperature under vacuum, so the structure, flavour and nutrients of the fresh food stay intact — unlike sun-drying or heat-drying, which cook out most of what made it good.

7
Stage
Process

Freeze-dried vs. everything else

Sun-drying and oven-drying use heat, which breaks down vitamins and flattens flavour. Freeze-drying uses cold and vacuum instead — the ice inside the food turns straight to vapour, leaving the shape and nutrients behind.

Source to shelf, in seven steps

Every batch follows the same path — the one we'd want to see if it were our own family's food.

01
Source
Fruit and vegetables graded at the farm gate by our partner growers in [region, Nepal — placeholder].
02
Preparation
Washed, sorted and cut by hand within hours of arrival to preserve freshness.
03
Freezing
Rapidly frozen to lock cell structure in place before drying begins.
04
Vacuum Drying
Ice sublimates directly to vapour under controlled vacuum — no heat, no shrinkage.
05
Quality Control
Every batch tested and logged — moisture content, appearance, taste — before packaging.
06
Packaging
Sealed against moisture and oxygen, and batch-coded so every pack is traceable.
07
Delivery
To your door, a retailer's shelf, or a distributor's warehouse.
